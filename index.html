Read before you begin

This library is for general education, not diagnosis or individualized treatment. Move gently and stay within a comfortable range. Stop if a movement causes sharp pain, worsening pain traveling into an arm or leg, numbness, tingling, weakness, dizziness, or loss of balance. Ask a qualified healthcare professional which exercises are appropriate after surgery, trauma, or a new diagnosis.

Stop and seek medical care

Do not rely on a stretch library when symptoms may indicate a serious injury or neurological problem. Seek urgent or emergency care as appropriate for:

  • Loss of bladder or bowel control
  • Numbness in the groin or saddle area
  • New or progressive limb weakness
  • Chest pain or trouble breathing
  • Severe pain after a fall, collision, or other trauma
  • Fever with severe neck or back pain
  • A hot, red, markedly swollen joint
  • Inability to bear weight or use the limb
